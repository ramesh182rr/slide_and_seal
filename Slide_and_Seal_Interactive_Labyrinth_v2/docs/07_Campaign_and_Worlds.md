# 07 — Campaign and Seven World Bible

## Campaign structure

50 handcrafted levels:
- 30 free.
- 20 premium.
- Seven worlds.
- Daily Puzzle is a separate free mode.

## World 1 — The Awakening (Levels 1–6)
**Theme:** ancient stone machinery, moss, soft cyan energy.  
**Teaches:** maze reading, core route, run/resume, safe checkpoints.  
**Landmark:** first dormant seal.

## World 2 — Gravity Works (Levels 7–12)
**Theme:** blue crystal chambers and suspended platforms.  
**Teaches:** gravity direction, gravity zones, settling and blockers.  
**Landmark:** gravity reactor.

## World 3 — The Push Protocol (Levels 13–18)
**Theme:** amber industrial mechanisms and pressure machinery.  
**Teaches:** selected-object push, push lanes, barriers and platform positioning.  
**Landmark:** pressure cannon.

## World 4 — Portal Nexus (Levels 19–24)
**Theme:** violet portals and spatial architecture.  
**Teaches:** paired portals, entry/exit orientation, blocked exits and routing.  
**Landmark:** portal stabilizer.

## World 5 — The Sealed Chambers (Levels 25–30)
**Theme:** gold-lit gates, energy receivers and sacred machinery.  
**Teaches:** pressure switches, toggles, gate dependencies and energy routing.  
**Landmark:** central seal.

## World 6 — The Shifting Labyrinth (Levels 31–40, premium)
**Theme:** rotating platforms, magnetic structures, deep blue and red energy.  
**Teaches:** rotating sections, polarity and combinations.  
**Landmark:** shifting core chamber.

## World 7 — The Final Machine (Levels 41–50, premium)
**Theme:** monumental violet/cyan machine, layered floating structures.  
**Teaches:** all mechanics in multi-stage sequences.  
**Landmark:** final seal engine.

## Level progression principles

- Introduce one new mechanic at a time.
- Use a safe demonstration level.
- Follow with a single-mechanic puzzle.
- Then combine with previously learned mechanics.
- Avoid introducing multiple new rules in one level.
- Premium difficulty comes from deeper dependencies, not arbitrary clutter.
- Never require reflexes or real-time timing.

## Campaign star system

- One star: complete.
- Two stars: meet designer action target.
- Three stars: verified optimal or best-known solution.
- Stars never block campaign progression.
